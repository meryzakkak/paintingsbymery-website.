Art as my therapy
About Mery

Before I had the words, I had the colors.

My mother painted, and her brushstrokes were the first colors I ever knew.

Colors made sense when words didn’t. I left Lebanon in 2018 thinking it would be for one year. It became much more. Leaving home was an emotional fracture, and painting began to hold me: not as a formal practice, but as a survival instinct. The canvas travelled with me.

In the UAE the canvas became my medicine. Every painting is slow, intentional and personal: an invitation to pause, feel and reconnect.
