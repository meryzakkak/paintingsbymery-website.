The small print

Privacy policy

What information this website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

This website, paintingsuae.com, belongs to Mery Zakkak, an artist based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, who sells her original paintings and runs art workshops as Paintings by Mery and MeryCreativeJourney. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Mery. You can reach us at merycreativejourney@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +971 56 338 0577.

The short version

  • This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no online payments.
  • We only get your name and contact details if you choose to message us.
  • We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site.
  • We never sell your information.

Information you give us

When you contact us by WhatsApp, email or Instagram about a painting, a commission or a workshop, we receive what you send: usually your name, phone number or email address, and your message. If you buy a painting or book a workshop, we may also keep:

  • your delivery address, if you ask for a painting to be delivered or shipped;
  • a record of your purchase or booking and your bank transfer payment;
  • for children’s workshops, a parent’s contact details and anything you tell us about your child, such as their name and age.

We use this only to reply to you, arrange your purchase, delivery or workshop place, and keep the records we need for our accounts. We don’t add you to a mailing list unless you ask to join one. You can leave the WhatsApp community at any time.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, two analytics tools collect information about your visit:

  • Google Analytics records things like the pages you view, how you found the site, your approximate location (country and city), your device and browser, and when you click a WhatsApp, email or Instagram link.
  • Microsoft Clarity records how you scroll, click and move around the pages, so we can see heatmaps and replays of visits. These show how the site is used; they don’t tell us who you are.

Both tools use cookies or similar technologies, and they process this information under their own privacy policies: Google and Microsoft. We look at this information in summary to improve the site and see which paintings and workshops people are interested in. We don’t use it for advertising.

Other services the site uses

  • Hosting: the site is hosted by Netlify, whose servers handle your visit and may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address.
  • Fonts: the site’s fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when a page opens.
  • Videos: some films are hosted on YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play; after that, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Links: links to Instagram, WhatsApp and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Cookies and your choices

The cookies on this site come from Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. The site also remembers in your browser whether you muted the music, so it stays muted on your next visit. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works fully without them.

Photos from workshops

We sometimes share photos and videos from workshops on this site and on Instagram. We don’t publish faces of participants without their permission, and children’s faces are always blurred or cropped out. If you’d like a photo of you or your child removed, just tell us and we’ll take it down.

Sharing your information

We don’t sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share it when we need to: for example, giving your address to a courier to deliver your painting, or when the law requires it. The services named above may process information outside the UAE.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and contact details for as long as we need them to help you, and purchase and payment records for as long as we need them for our accounts and any legal requirements. Analytics data is kept for the period set in each tool and then deleted automatically.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Contact us using the details below and we’ll reply as soon as we can.

Children

This site is meant for adults. Children’s workshops are booked by a parent or guardian, and we don’t knowingly collect information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date below.

Questions

For anything about your privacy, contact merycreativejourney@gmail.com or WhatsApp +971 56 338 0577. See also our Terms & copyright.
