Buying an original painting is different from buying a print. There is only one, someone made it by hand, and it changes how a room feels every time you walk in. As a painter based in Abu Dhabi, I get asked the same questions again and again, so here is everything I tell people before they choose.

### 1. Start with the wall, not the painting

Measure the wall before you fall in love with anything. A painting over a sofa or a bed usually looks best at about two-thirds of the furniture's width. A painting that is too small floats; one that is too big crowds the room.

For a narrow wall, a hallway or a staircase, think tall and slim, or a set of small pieces. My [Family Bond](/paintings/family-bond/) set was made for exactly that: four pieces you can hang together or apart.

### 2. Look at the light

Abu Dhabi light is strong. Keep a painting out of direct sun, which fades colour over the years, and away from AC vents and bathrooms, where heat and humidity change quickly. Soft daylight or a warm lamp at night is where acrylic colours come alive.

### 3. Choose a feeling, then a colour

Ask yourself how you want the room to feel. Calm? Blues and sea tones, like [Lost in the City](/paintings/lost-in-the-city/). Warm and full of life? Golds, reds and oranges, like the paintings in my Fluid Emotions series. Every painting on my site has a "Perfect for" line to help with this.

### 4. Set a budget that fits

Original art does not have to be expensive. My paintings start at AED 50 for small pieces and go up to AED 4,500 for large statement works, and selected paintings from 2024 and earlier are 40% off until Valentine's Day, 14 February 2027. Small originals make thoughtful gifts; a large piece can be the heart of a living room.

### 5. See it in person

A photo never shows the texture of the paint, the beads or the gold leaf. If you are in Abu Dhabi, you can visit my studio in the Zayed Sports City area and see a painting before you decide. Just [message me on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/971563380577) to arrange a time.

### 6. Can't find the right one? Commission it

If you have a size, a colour palette or a story in mind, I can paint it for you. Send me a photo of the wall and a few words about what the painting should hold, and we will shape it together.

### 7. Payment, delivery and care

- **Payment:** by bank transfer. The details are shared when you confirm your painting.
- **Delivery:** collect it in Abu Dhabi, or ask for delivery anywhere in the UAE or international shipping, with a quote before you pay.
- **Care:** dust gently with a soft dry cloth. No water, no cleaning sprays.
