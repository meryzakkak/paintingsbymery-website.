When I left Lebanon in 2018, I didn't have the words for what I was feeling. I had paint. Colour by colour, painting became the way I made sense of leaving home, and it is the reason I wrote my memoir, [Art as My Therapy](/art-as-my-therapy.pdf). Today I share that practice with others in my creative art expression sessions in Abu Dhabi.

### Who it is for

Anyone who wants to slow down, feel and reconnect, especially people who say "I can't draw". You don't need any experience. Nothing you make is judged, graded or compared. People come during a change in their lives, after a hard year, or simply because they miss making something with their hands.

### What happens in a session

1. **Arrive and settle.** We start with a few quiet minutes to breathe and leave the day outside.
2. **A gentle starting point.** I give a simple prompt: a word, a memory, a colour that matches how you feel today.
3. **Paint.** You work with acrylics, fluid paint, texture and your hands. The focus is on the process, not on a perfect result.
4. **Look and reflect.** At the end we look at what appeared on the paper or canvas. You share only what you want to share.

You leave with your artwork and, most of the time, a lighter feeling.

### Three ways to join

- **A single session**, to try it.
- **One month**, to build a habit.
- **The full 90-day (12-week) journey**, for deeper change.

You can start whenever you are ready.

### Is this art therapy?

I hold a Training Diploma in Art Therapy (2025) from Albyan Institute Education Support Services in Abu Dhabi. My sessions use art as a reflective, therapeutic practice. They are not a replacement for medical or psychological care: if you are going through something serious, please also speak to a qualified mental health professional.

### For groups and teams

The same approach works for friends, celebrations and corporate wellbeing days, across the UAE or online. Kids from age 6 can join my kids' art workshops, where they explore colour, texture and play.
